PART 1: IN-DEPTH TECHNICAL ANALYSIS OF EXISTING CODEBASE
================================================================================

1.1 PACKAGE FOLDER (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/packages/` & `src/components/packages/`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• `new/page.js` & `[id]/edit/page.js`:
    - Structured as a 7-stage guided wizard:
      1. Basics (Title, Destinations, Nights, Days [auto: Nights + 1], Highlights, Cover Photo)
      2. Itinerary (Day-by-Day schedule via ItineraryBuilder)
      3. Accommodation (Multi-tier hotel selection via AccommodationPanel)
      4. Vehicle (Fleet & transport rates via VehiclePanel)
      5. Pricing (Cost + Margin + GST + Per Person / Couple via PricingPanel)
      6. Instructions (Policies, rules & terms via InstructionPanel)
      7. Review (Comprehensive summary + Live Reactive Sidebar + Save Draft/Publish)
    - Real-time Sidebar Calculator: Displays live totals, per-couple rates, per-person rates,
      and vehicle specs as inputs change.
    - Hydration Safety: Uses `suppressHydrationWarning` and `immediatelyRender: false`.

• `ItineraryBuilder.jsx` & `DescriptionEditorDialog.jsx`:
    - Auto-suggests city activities from `/api/activities?city={cityId}`.
    - Integrates Tiptap v3 rich-text editor modal supporting:
      • Bold, Italics, Underline
      • Bullet points (•) with nested sub-bullets (circles, squares)
      • Numbered lists (1, 2, 3), Alphabetical (a, b, c), and Roman numerals (i, ii, iii)
      • Tab / Shift+Tab keyboard shortcuts + Indent/Outdent buttons
      • Direct clipboard pasting from Word, Google Docs, WhatsApp, and PDFs
    - Meal inclusion checkboxes (`breakfast`, `lunch`, `dinner`).

• `AccommodationPanel.jsx`:
    - Multi-tier category support (Option 1: Standard, Option 2: Deluxe, Option 3: Luxury).
    - City-aware hotel & room picker with meal plans (EP, CP, MAP, AP) and star ratings (1–5★).
    - Per-option custom profit margin overrides (Absolute ₹ or Percentage %).

• `VehiclePanel.jsx`:
    - Fleet categories: Sedan (4S), SUV (6S), MUV (7S), Tempo Traveller (12S), Mini Bus, Bus.
    - AC vs Non-AC toggles, pricing, driver allowance, toll/parking presets.

• `PricingPanel.jsx`:
    - Financial calculation engine:
      • Subtotal = Accommodation Base + Transport Fleet + Itinerary Activities
      • Profit Margin = Flat ₹ or % of Subtotal
      • Optional GST Tax (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%)
      • Grand Total rounded to nearest ₹100
      • Per Couple Price (`finalPrice ÷ pax × 2`) & Per Person Price (`finalPrice ÷ pax`)

• `[id]/page.js` (Package Preview):
    - Full-bleed cinematic hero banner with cover photo.
    - Timeline-style itinerary (`D1`, `D2`, `D3`) with meal badges and photo gallery.
    - Tabbed accommodation tier switcher and transport specs card.

1.2 ACCOMMODATION & ACTIVITY FOLDERS (`src/components/accommodation/` & `src/components/activities/`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Accommodation Subsystem:
    - Multi-city hierarchical database: `City` ➔ `Hotel` ➔ `Room`.
    - Season Calendar support for peak/off-peak price multipliers.
• Activity & Direct Booking Subsystem:
    - Standalone city-based activity catalog with pricing tiers (`adultPrice`, `childPrice`).
    - QR Code Ticketing: `ActivityVoucherModal.jsx` generates high-resolution SVG QR codes.
    - Public Verification: `/vouchers/[code]` allows instant mobile camera scanning & verification.
    - Dashboard Verification: `VerifyVoucherModal.jsx` for quick staff lookup.

1.3 BACKEND & SANITIZATION (`src/lib/sanitizePackage.js` & `src/models/Package.js`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• `PackageSchema` (`src/models/Package.js`):
    - Self-contained embedded document containing destinations, itinerary, accommodationOptions,
      vehicle, pricing, instructions, and activities.
    - Pre-save hook auto-derives `days = nights + 1` and creates the route string (`Munnar → Alleppey`).
• `sanitizePackage.js`:
    - Strict coercion: parses integers, cleans array items, preserves rich HTML descriptions.

PART 2: ARCHITECTURAL BLUEPRINT FOR "CREATE QUOTATION" COMPONENT
================================================================================

Why a dedicated Quotation component is needed:
While "Travel Packages" are generic templates for the public website, a "Quotation"
is a personalized commercial proposal customized for a specific lead/client with
their specific dates, guest count, custom markup, and client contact info.

RECOMMENDED KEY FEATURES FOR THE QUOTATION BUILDER:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Dual Creation Workflow & 1-Click Package Fetch:
   • Option A — "Fetch & Clone from Travel Package":
     - Browse the package catalog or click "+ Create Quote" directly on any package card.
     - Performs a DEEP-CLONE of the master package (Itinerary, Hotels, Vehicle, Pricing, Policies).
     - The original master package remains completely untouched and safe.
     - Travel Agent can immediately modify ANYTHING the client doesn't want:
       * Replace any hotel property or room type (e.g. swap 3★ for 5★).
       * Add, delete, or re-order day activities and sightseeing passes.
       * Upgrade/downgrade vehicle (Sedan ➔ Innova Crysta ➔ Tempo Traveller).
       * Edit day descriptions with rich sub-bullets and Roman numerals.
       * Set custom client margins, special discounts, and customized payment terms.
   • Option B — "Build Custom Quotation from Scratch":
     - Build a unique tour itinerary, hotel tiers, and transport tailored for custom requests.
   • Option C — "Post-Creation Re-Edit Studio":
     - Reopen any existing quotation at `/dashboard/quotations/[id]/edit` anytime to revise
       hotels, dates, or discounts based on client calls/WhatsApp feedback.

2. Client & Lead Management Header:
   • Client Name, WhatsApp Phone Number, Email, and City.
   • Lead Source (Website, WhatsApp, Referral, Walk-in, B2B Partner).
   • Quotation Ref Number (e.g. `QT-2026-8492` or `MANDATE-QT-014`).
   • Quotation Validity Expiry Date (e.g. "Valid for 7 days").

3. Dynamic Passenger & Room Configuration:
   • Number of Adults (12+ yrs), Children with Bed, Children without Bed, Infants.
   • Total Rooms required (e.g. 2 Double Rooms + 1 Extra Bed).
   • Instant recalculation of room counts and vehicle capacity recommendations.

4. Multi-Category Client Options (Tiers):
   • Give the customer choices in the same quotation:
     - Tier 1: Standard (3★ Hotels + CP Breakfast)
     - Tier 2: Deluxe (4★ Resorts + MAP Dinner & Breakfast)
     - Tier 3: Luxury (5★ / Heritage Suite + MAP/AP Meals)

5. Commercial Margin & Discount Engine:
   • Base Net Cost (Hotel + Vehicle + Activities + Guides).
   • Custom Profit Margin (Flat ₹ or %).
   • Client Special Discount (e.g. ₹2,000 off to close deal).
   • GST Tax calculation (5% with ITC, 12%, or Exempt).
   • Per Couple, Per Person, and Total Quotation Value.

6. Client Deliverables & Sharing (3 Modes):
   • Mode 1: Branded PDF Itinerary Export with agency logo, terms, and contact.
   • Mode 2: Online Interactive Quotation Webpage (`/quote/[refCode]`) with "Accept & Pay Advance" button.
   • Mode 3: One-Click WhatsApp Formatted Text Copy for direct messaging.

PART 3: DATABASE SCHEMA SPECIFICATION FOR QUOTATIONS
================================================================================

File: `src/models/Quotation.js` (Enhanced Full Schema)

```javascript
const QuotationSchema = new mongoose.Schema(
  {
    // Identification
    quotationCode: { type: String, unique: true, required: true, uppercase: true }, // e.g. QT-2026-8492
    sourcePackage: { type: mongoose.Schema.Types.ObjectId, ref: "Package", default: null }, // Cloned package ID

    // Client & Lead Info
    client: {
      name:        { type: String, required: true, trim: true },
      phone:       { type: String, required: true, trim: true },
      email:       { type: String, trim: true, lowercase: true },
      company:     { type: String, trim: true, default: "" }, // For B2B agents
      clientType:  { type: String, enum: ["b2c", "b2b", "corporate"], default: "b2c" },
      leadSource:  { type: String, default: "Direct" },
    },

    // Trip & Travel Dates
    tripDetails: {
      title:        { type: String, required: true, trim: true },
      startDate:    { type: Date, required: true },
      endDate:      { type: Date, required: true },
      nights:       { type: Number, required: true, min: 1 },
      days:         { type: Number, required: true, min: 1 },
      destinations: [DestinationSchema], // Multi-city route
      destination:  { type: String, default: "" }, // Derived route string
    },

    // Guests & Rooms
    passengers: {
      adults:            { type: Number, default: 2, min: 1 },
      childrenWithBed:   { type: Number, default: 0, min: 0 },
      childrenNoBed:     { type: Number, default: 0, min: 0 },
      infants:           { type: Number, default: 0, min: 0 },
      totalRooms:        { type: Number, default: 1, min: 1 },
    },

    // Day-by-Day Itinerary (Rich HTML Description, Meals, Activities)
    itinerary: [ItineraryDaySchema],

    // Multi-Tier Hotel Categories (Standard, Deluxe, Luxury)
    accommodationOptions: [AccommodationOptionSchema],
    selectedOptionIndex:  { type: Number, default: 0 },

    // Transport Fleet
    vehicle: VehicleSchema,

    // Pricing, Margin & Taxes
    pricing: {
      baseAccommodation: { type: Number, default: 0 },
      vehicleCost:       { type: Number, default: 0 },
      activitiesCost:    { type: Number, default: 0 },
      subtotalNetCost:   { type: Number, default: 0 },
      marginType:        { type: String, enum: ["absolute", "percentage"], default: "absolute" },
      marginValue:       { type: Number, default: 0 },
      discountAmount:    { type: Number, default: 0 },
      includeGst:        { type: Boolean, default: false },
      gstPercentage:     { type: Number, default: 5 },
      finalQuotationAmount: { type: Number, required: true },
      perPersonPrice:    { type: Number, default: 0 },
      perCouplePrice:    { type: Number, default: 0 },
      currency:          { type: String, default: "INR" },
    },

    // Inclusions, Exclusions & Payment Terms
    inclusions:   [{ type: String }],
    exclusions:   [{ type: String }],
    paymentTerms: {
      advancePercentage: { type: Number, default: 25 },
      advanceAmount:     { type: Number, default: 0 },
      balanceDueDate:    { type: Date },
      termsText:         { type: String, default: "25% advance to confirm, balance 15 days before travel." },
    },
    instructions: [InstructionBlockSchema],

    // Lifecycle Status
    status: {
      type: String,
      enum: ["draft", "sent", "viewed", "confirmed", "expired", "cancelled"],
      default: "draft",
    },
    validUntil: { type: Date, required: true }, // Expiration date

    createdBy: { type: mongoose.Schema.Types.ObjectId, ref: "User", required: true },
  },
  { timestamps: true }
);
```

PART 5: REAL-TIME GRANULAR CUSTOMIZATION & EDIT ENGINE
================================================================================

Whenever a client requests changes (e.g. "Upgrade Munnar hotel to 5-star",
"Add Elephant Safari on Day 2", "Change vehicle to Innova Crysta", "Add 1 Extra Night"),
the Quotation Builder allows complete, live, flexible customization:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 ACTIVITY CUSTOMIZATION & OPTIONAL ADD-ONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Day-Specific Activity Customizer:
    - Click "+ Add / Select Activities" on any individual day.
    - Search & filter city-specific activities (e.g. Kathakali Show, Tea Museum, Speedboat).
    - Override activity price (e.g. mark an activity as "Complimentary / Free" or set custom price).
    - Remove or re-order activities on any day with 1 click.
• Optional Client Add-ons:
    - Include optional sightseeing/experience passes with individual price tags
      so clients can choose whether to include them in the final quote.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 ACCOMMODATION & HOTEL TIER CUSTOMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Per-Night Hotel Swapper:
    - Change hotel for any specific night (e.g. Night 1 & 2 in Resort A, Night 3 in Resort B).
    - Change room category on the fly (e.g. Standard Room ➔ Private Pool Villa).
    - Switch meal plans per stay (EP: Room Only, CP: Breakfast, MAP: Breakfast+Dinner, AP: All Meals).
• Rate Overrides & Seasonal Customization:
    - Directly edit the price-per-night to match custom negotiated agent rates.
    - Custom margin per hotel tier (e.g. 10% on 3★ Standard, 15% on 5★ Luxury).
• Duration & Night Adjustments:
    - Add an extra night to any city or remove a night (auto-recalculates total days & dates).
    - Add unlimited custom accommodation tiers (e.g. Tier 1: 3-Star, Tier 2: 4-Star, Tier 3: 5-Star).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 VEHICLE & FLEET CUSTOMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Vehicle Category & Fleet Upgrades:
    - Switch vehicle type anytime: Sedan ➔ SUV ➔ Innova Crysta ➔ Tempo Traveller (12/17/26 Seats).
    - Switch AC / Non-AC preferences.
    - Adjust seating capacity based on family/group size.
• Custom Logistics & Driver Terms:
    - Custom vehicle total pricing for special routes or multi-city transfers.
    - Toggle transport inclusions: Fuel, Driver Allowance, Parking, Toll Taxes, Interstate Permits.
    - Add multiple vehicles for large groups/delegations (e.g. 2 Innovas for 10 Pax).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4 DAY-BY-DAY ITINERARY CUSTOMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Reorder & Custom Day Editing:
    - Add custom stopovers, sightseeing routes, and leisure days.
    - Edit Day Titles (e.g. "Day 3: Scenic Drive to Thekkady & Wildlife Boat Safari").
    - Rich Text Description Editor: Add paragraphs, nested sub-bullets (•, circles, squares),
      numerical points (1, 2, 3), and Roman numerals (i, ii, iii).
    - Individual meal toggles per day (`breakfast`, `lunch`, `dinner`).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.5 REAL-TIME COST & PROFIT MARGIN RECALCULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
As soon as ANY activity, hotel, room, or vehicle is customized:
    1. Base Subtotal recalculates instantly:
       `Subtotal = Accommodation Base + Vehicle Fleet + Activities Total`
    2. Profit Margin recalculates dynamically (Flat ₹ or %):
       `Margin = MarginType === 'percentage' ? (Subtotal × Margin%) ÷ 100 : Margin₹`
    3. Optional GST & Taxes apply automatically (e.g. 5% GST):
       `GST = IncludeGST ? (Subtotal + Margin) × 5% : 0`
    4. Client Special Discount applies:
       `Grand Total = (Subtotal + Margin + GST) - Discount`
    5. Divided Unit Rates update live on screen:
       `Per Couple Rate = (Grand Total ÷ Pax) × 2`
       `Per Person Rate = Grand Total ÷ Pax`

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.6 FULL POST-CREATION EDIT STUDIO & REVISION TRACKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Live Quotation Revisions (`/dashboard/quotations/[id]/edit`):
    - Travel agents can reopen any previously sent quotation and adjust hotels, dates,
      passenger count, or discounts based on client negotiation.
    - Click "Update & Resend Quotation" to instantly update the client's live web link
      and generate a revised PDF / WhatsApp summary without changing the quote URL.
